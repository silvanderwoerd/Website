We're thankful to announce that Broken Wings has won its next award, this time at the 4th edition of Ponme Un Corto 2024 produced by Kika Kills Producciones with the collaboration of the Huerta de Rey City Council.

‍

This win has automatically made Broken Wings an official selection at the upcoming Cineco Festival of Cinema and Ecology happening in December 2024.
