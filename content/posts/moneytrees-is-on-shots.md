# "**The environmental organisation shines a light on the unscrupulous banking practices destroying our forests in an impactful campaign created by Studio Birthplace.**" - Shots

‍

MoneyTrees has been featured on Shots.

‍

An excerpt from the article:

‍

Co-directed by Sil Van Der Woerd and Jorik Dozy through Studio Birthplace, environmental organisation Greenpeace has today launched this shocking music-video-style campaign exposing the environmental impact of unregulated banking.

‍

Titled MoneyTrees, the film opens with an orgiastic portrayal of greedy bankers destroying a lush rainforest with their chainsaws in their relentless pursuit of profit. Meanwhile, government officials look the other way - their inaction further exacerbating the crisis.

‍
