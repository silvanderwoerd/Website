# Launched by Rainforest Partnership in 2010, Films for the Forest **connects people with rainforests through the powerful medium of film.**

‍

Earth Defender has won under the Narrative Shorts category at the 2025/2026 Films for the Forest Short Film Challange.

‍

‍

Films for the Forest is an annual juried short film collection that uses the power of creative storytelling to convene, empower, and activate global audiences to take action on behalf of the world's forests.

‍

See all the awards and winners [here](https://www.filmsfortheforest.org/).
