# We are excited to welcome 3 new powerhouse members to the Studio Birthplace team!

‍

From left to right, learn about them below and their roles in Studio Birthplace.

‍

### Liv Merry - Manager Strategic Partnerships

‍

"Studio Birthplace has been a dream collaborator and partner for some time, and I am thrilled to be supporting them on some incredible project. The films and campaigns that we have ahead are not only creatively inspiring, but are so rich on impact they promise to leave a lasting impact on our audience. I can't wait to see the magic unfold and to be part in bringing these stories to life."

‍

### Dianne Hummal - Global Expansion Director

‍<br>"When I first encountered the work by Studio Birthplace, it moved me so much that I knew I wanted to help carry the message. As Global Expansion Director, I’m on a mission to help businesses, NGOs, VCs and other collectives tell urgent stories together with Studio Birthplace. I encourage everyone to dig through their skills and talents to figure out ways how you can contribute to make a change."

‍

### Kay Vasey - Head of Philanthropy

‍

"As Head of Philanthropy, I am really looking forward to introducing the Studio Birthplace team to supporters and funders of their highly impactful work. I previously commissioned the team to create (PLASTIK), a film focused on catalysing action towards ending the plastic pollution crisis in Southeast Asia, which has gone on to win multiple international awards. I wholeheartedly believe in the power of Studio Birthplace’s work to move hearts and mind."
