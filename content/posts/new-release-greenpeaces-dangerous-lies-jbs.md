### Out now - Greenpeace's Dangerous Lies: JBS

‍

*Greenpeace's Dangerous Lies: JBS* is about JBS, the world's largest meat producer, who has pledged to be carbon neutral by 2040.

‍

But their plan to do so, allows them to keep destroying the valuable Amazon Rainforest and does not address the fundamental source of their climate impact: methane release by cattle.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/65e765876bec1b1c4e28a9fb_content1.newrelease.dangerouslies1.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

Dangerous Lies is a campaign aiming to expose 'carbon offset schemes' by big polluters.

‍

Through short live-action segments where the concept of greenwashing is depicted, and using educational animation, we lift the veil on the marketing scams these companies apply to keep polluting our planet and turning nature into a commodity.
