# Requiem 2019 sees the last remaining blue whale coming eye to eye with its only enemy; mankind.

‍

Requiem 2019 premiered at Aarhus Festuge in Aarhus, Denmark. The film was directed by Rutger Hauer and Sil van der Woerd who felt an urge to bring attention to the ongoing issue of whaling and support anti whaling organisation Sea Shepherd.

‍

The premiere was accompanied with a live performance of Jane Blue, who recorded the soundtrack for the film.

‍

‍

Rutger Hauer has been closely involved with Sea Shepherd for many years as an Honorary Board Member.

*“This is why I look upon Requiem 2019 as a gift to Sea Shepherd.”*
