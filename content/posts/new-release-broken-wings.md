### Out now: '*Broken Wings'* is a unique documentary-music film that unveils the untold story of bird poaching in Southeast Asia.

‍

*Broken Wings* captures the journey tropical birds make from forest to cage, revealing how forests are emptied on a massive scale, threatening more than 1,500 bird species with extinction. Yet almost nobody knows about it.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/65dfef0f5b0ca4cf05d15993_Content1.NewRelease.BrokenImage.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

You might have never thought about it, but where does your pet bird come from?

‍

Bird poaching is a global problem that has devastating consequences for ecosystems and biodiversity. Southeast Asia is home to over one fifth of global bird species.

‍

This richness has brought about large-scale wildlife trafficking throughout the region to fulfill demand for the pet trade.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/65dfef6043ba3084890d67e2_Content2.NewRelease.BrokenImage.jpg"></div></figure>

Globally, 49% of bird species are declining, one in eight are threatened with extinction.

**​**

In an effort to raise awareness on this critical issue, nature focused *WaterBear Network* and *Studio Birthplace* teamed up with renowned composer *Ludovico Einaudi* to tell this story.
