# "November 25 was the *shots* Awards EMEA ceremony at which a host of people and companies from across Europe were celebrated as best in class across the last 24 months."

‍

We are very happy to have received a Shots Award Nomination for Ad of the Year in category of Charity for Greenpeace's Wasteminster.

‍

We were nominated alongside Face à L'inceste, International Paralympic Committee, The Alzheimer's Society and others.

‍
