# Music video Birthplace won Independent Video of the Year at Independent Music Awards in UK.

‍

We're incredibly honored to have received such an award.

‍

The AIM Independent Music Awards, hosted by the Association of Independent Music (commonly abbreviated to AIM), began in 2011. It is established through a collection of independent record labels and providing service and support to independent and smaller artists and record labels globally, achieving recognition for their efforts and works.
