## **Woolmark dresses up synthetic garments as zombie-like invasion - Campaign**

Our film for The Woolmark Company's 'Wear Wool, Not Waste' is Campaign's Ad of the Day.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/66f19f94083f8cec534f487c_66f19f73026ca04be437b307_Screenshot%25202024-09-23%2520140332.png"></div></figure>

‍

An excerpt from the article:

‍

The film dramatises the long-lasting impact of synthetic garments by portraying them as a "zombie invasion", symbolising how these materials continue to haunt the planet.

‍

Synthetic fabrics, which were introduced fewer than 200 years ago, are predicted to make up 73% of global fibre production by 2030.

‍

Many polyester products, a common synthetic material, can persist in landfills for more than 200 years.

‍
