# Greenpeace's Wasteminster has won 2 Golds at the British Arrows Awards 2022.

‍

We're ecstatic to announce that Wasteminster, made for Greenpeace, has won two 2 British Arrows, specifically Gold for Sustainable Impact Award and Gold for Animation (CGI).

‍

Massive thanks to all the team involved who crafted this campaign with passion, expertise and ingenuity. The recognition is fantastic and the fact the work resulted in helping plastic waste change is an achievement we are also so incredibly proud of.
