# "Prepare to be shocked. In this eye-opening interview, directors [**Jorik Dozy**](https://www.instagram.com/jorikdozy/) and [Sil van der Woerd](https://www.instagram.com/silvanderwoerd/?hl=en) reveal the hidden cost of your investments. A powerful weapon in the fight against environmental destruction, “MoneyTrees” is more than just a film—it’s an urgent call to action." - The Vale Magazine

‍

MoneyTrees directors Sil van der Woerd and Jorik Dozy have been interviewed by Vale Magazine.

An excerpt from the interview: 

‍

Q: The film addresses a global issue. How did you ensure the film’s message would be universally understood?

A: We focused on a simple yet powerful concept: showing businesspeople in suits using tools to directly destroy nature. They celebrate their profits while governments look the other way. In the film, they laugh in boardroom meetings as the forests burn because of their actions. We portray their complete indifference—it’s a tragic but true story of limitless greed. The story is told visually and simply, so everyone can grasp the injustice at play.
