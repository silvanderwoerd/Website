# Earth Defender will be competing alongside nominees FKA Twigs and Geordie Greep for Best Alternative Video.

‍

Earth Defender has been nominated for Best Alternative Video UK at the UK Music Video Awards.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/68e38752e657ece2cadb10f9_Screenshot%202025-10-06%20060440.png" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

Since 2008, The UK Music Video Awards has been an annual celebration of creativity, technical excellence and innovation in music video and moving image for music.

‍

We’re proud to return to the UKMVAs with Novo Amor again after our music video for his work Repeat Until Death was nominated in the same category back in 2019.

‍
