# The Journey of Hope has been selected to be part of The World Health Organization's Film Festival: Health for All.

‍

We are very proud to have Sapna's story be seen in the World health Organization's film festival: Health For All.

‍

We are grateful that her memory is able to live on through avenues like this and more people can experience a little bit of what her life was like and learn from it.

‍

The WHO Health for All Film Festival invites independent filmmakers, production companies, public institutions, NGOs, communities, students, and film schools from around the world to submit their original short films on health.
