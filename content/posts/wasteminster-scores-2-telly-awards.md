# Greenpeace's Wasteminster has won 2 Telly Awards.

‍

We're ecstatic to announce that Wasteminster, made for Greenpeace, has won two Telly Awards in the Online Commercials category: Gold for Use of 3D Animation and Silver for Social Impact.

‍

‍

The Telly Awards, founded in 1979, annually showcases the best work created within television and across video, for all screens.

‍

The Telly Awards receives over 12,000 entries across 5 continents, so to have been recognized is a huge honor. Thank you to our collaborators and team for their hard work.
