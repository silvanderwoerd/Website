## Out now - Greenpeace's 'Oilympics' is an animated film calling out how giant fossil fuel corporation Eni is using the Winter Olympics & Paralympics to greenwash its climate destruction.

‍

##### *'Oilympics'* is our latest collaboration with Greenpeace, and a continuation of the work we started with TotalPollution by highlighting how major sporting moments are being used to greenwash environmental destruction.

##### ‍

##### Eni, one of the world’s biggest oil and gas corporations, is using the Winter Olympics & Paralympics to greenwash its fossil-fuelled destruction.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/698201aa873d9790e45f025b_Oilympics_2.1.1-optimized.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

##### Eni’s annual fossil fuel emissions could melt enough glacier ice to fill 2.5 million Olympic swimming pools. Meanwhile, more than half of the potential host cities for Winter Games will become "climate-unreliable" by the 2080s due to the climate crisis.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6982019fcc5a39e96efad237_Oilympics_2.7.3-optimized.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

##### Through this film, Greenpeace is calling for the International Olympic Committee to drop all oil and gas sponsorship.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/69820200c421e0776508edce_Oilympics_2.13.1-thumbnail.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

##### Winter sport needs snow, not fossil fuels.
