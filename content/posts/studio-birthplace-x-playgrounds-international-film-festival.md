# Studio Birthplace collaborates again with We Are Playgrounds through their presence at Playgrounds International Film Festival.

‍

Studio Birthplace was part of We are Playgrounds' International Film Festival (PIFF) this year, presenting two talks and participating in Q&A session during opening night, held at Natlab Film Theater in Eindhoven, The Netherlands.

‍

‍

Reflecting on their time at PIFF, Sil says:

‍

“In 2006, as a student, I won ‘Best Digital Short’ at the first-ever Playgrounds Festival. Now, Jorik and I are here speaking about the pro-planet work of Studio Birthplace. Playgrounds has given us a platform over the years, and through it, we’ve met so many inspiring artists and students.”
