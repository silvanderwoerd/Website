# The HollyShorts Film Festival is an annual Oscar-Qualifying independent short film festival held in the iconic TCL Chinese Theatre.

‍

We're honored to announce that Earth Defender has won Best Music Video at the Oscar-Qualifying Hollyshorts Festival 2025.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/68a771fe73572c6915336bc4_Screenshot%202025-08-21%20162224.png" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

The HollyShorts Film Festival is based in Los Angeles, California. Since its establishment in 2005, HollyShorts has grown into a premier platform for independent filmmakers worldwide.

‍

This award and recognition is not just for the film, but for the message at its core: Earth Defenders are putting everything on the line to protect our Earth’s vanishing wilderness — our life support system.

‍

We’re grateful to our crew, collaborators, and the festival jury at for helping amplify this urgent message.
