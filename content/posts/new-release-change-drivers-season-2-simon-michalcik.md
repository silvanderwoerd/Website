### Out now - 'Change Drivers Season 2, Episode 2: Šimon Michalčík'

‍

*Change Drivers Season 2: Episode 2* is led by Plant-for-the-Planet Czechia's Managing Director Šimon Michalčík.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/66df41a5762bdbf4b9c0347f_66df417ee1384a72a16210e3_CD.Simon.Still1.jpeg"></div></figure>

‍

In this episode, Šimon highlights the importance of ecosystem restoration through his work at Plant-for-the-Planet and how we have the space to grow a trillion more trees in the world.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/66df41a5762bdbf4b9c0347b_66df41886beb49fbf30517b8_CD.Simon.Still2.jpeg"></div></figure>

‍

*Change Drivers* is a mini-series created for Discovery's various channels highlighting people working on the front lines of solving critical problems the world faces today. From plastic pollution to climate change, the series aims to tell the stories of those who are putting everything on the line to find solutions to some of our society's most dire problems.

‍

The episode is currently on-air on Discovery Channel as well as TLC, Food Network, Travel Channel, Animal Planet and more.
