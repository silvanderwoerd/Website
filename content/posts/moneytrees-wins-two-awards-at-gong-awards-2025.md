## **Moneytrees Wins Two Awards at GONG Awards 2025**

‍

We're delighted to announce that Moneytrees has won Gold VFX and Best of Category: Craft - Entertainment and Branded Entertainment award at the GONG Awards 2025.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/679607390233547b8e52d38c_679600897fb0bbb85e856770_moneytrees.gongawards.Content1.jpeg"></div></figure>

‍

The Creative Circle (GONG) Awards by the Association of Advertising & Marketing Singapore (AAMS) is a beacon of artistic achievement in Singapore.  Heckler, VFX Partner for Moneytrees, were in attendance for the ceremony where they won multiple awards.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/679607390233547b8e52d38f_679606f528099e7106abb6cd_moneytrees.gongawards.Content2.jpeg"></div></figure>

‍

Studio Birthplace congratulates Heckler for an incredible night at the GONG Awards.
