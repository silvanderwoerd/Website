# Sil, Rutger Hauer & Sea Shepherd at Playgrounds Festival.

‍

Legendary actor Rutger Hauer (Blade Runner) and Sil van der Woerd presented their co-directed short film Requiem 2019 at Playgrounds Festival in Amsterdam.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/65e8edeb51b84df26ae7e2dd_content.requiem.playgrounds.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

The film was made to support anti-whaling organization Sea Shepherd, of which Rutger Hauer is an ambassador.
