# Sil van der Woerd represents Studio Birthplace in DayDream Believers' 'SeaStory' project, an educational toolkit for children between 10-14 years old about the deep sea and specifically how to inspire people to protect the ocean's future through storytelling.

‍

In this educational toolkit, students learn more about the deep sea and how its importance needs to be protected. In between sea creatures facts, Sil teaches students how to inspire others to protect the ocean's future in a 4-part series of videos on the filmmaking process from thinking about ideas to developing a story to storyboarding and finally pitching a film.
