# Wear Wool, Not Waste wins Silver for the Flying High - Commercials category at this year's 1.4 Awards

‍

Our campaign film Wear Wool, Not Waste made for the Woolmark Company has won its second award, this time Silver under the Flying High - Commercials category at this year's One Point Four Awards.
