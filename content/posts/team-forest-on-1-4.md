"In Team Forest, the forest becomes the pitch, its floor, trunks and canopy transformed into a vertical field of play as footballers and stunt performers move through the landscape with astonishing physicality. Created to harness the global pull of the game, the film reframes one of the planet’s most vital ecosystems as a shared home ground, calling for urgent support to protect and restore forests before the final whistle blows."
