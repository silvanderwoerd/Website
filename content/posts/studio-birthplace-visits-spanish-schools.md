# Sil van der Woerd got to visit Spanish schools to talk about environmental and humanitarian topics through film.

‍

As a part of Bilbao's Cine Invisible International Film Festival, Sil van der Woerd joined a tour visiting many high schools in Bilbao, to talk about environmental and humanitarian topics through the music videos Terraform and Birthplace.

‍

‍

The school kids prepared assignments and the film program and group discussions that followed left both the students and the hosts inspired.
