# **"Drastic images for drastic facts."**  

‍

MoneyTrees has been featured on PAGE, a German-based design magazine.

‍

A translated to English excerpt of the article below:

‍

⁣

"Studio Birthplace is known for its eye-catching Hollywood-quality spots that campaign against environmental destruction. It has drowned Boris Johnson in plastic waste , submerged people in petroleum and flooded a sports stadium with it.

Everything is visually powerful, striking and of stunning quality. After all, Sil van der Woerd and Jorik Dozy studied visual effects in Los Angeles and later shot music videos and made a career at Industrial Light & Magic, owned by Star Wars creator George Lucas."

‍

Read the full feature [here](https://page-online.de/kreation/wie-immer-grosses-kino-studio-birthplace-fuer-greenpeace/).

Previously PAGE Magazine had a 3x feature spread about Studio Birthplace in their 01.2024 issue, which included an interview with our studio's creative directors Jorik Dozy and Sil van der Woerd.
