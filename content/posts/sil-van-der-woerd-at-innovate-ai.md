# Sil van der Woerd gave a talk about using AI for good at the INNOVATE AI XL Meetup.

‍

##### Sil van der Woerd was recently invited as one of seven speakers at the Innovate AI XL Meetup held in Arnhem, The Netherlands, where he tackled the question of "Now that AI is here, how can we use it for good?"

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/69e243baaf7c8c06c310a620_Sil-InnovateAI-Photo.png" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

##### The meetup brought together entrepreneurs, designers, and innovators to explore how AI is applied across sectors like health, energy, and the creative industries, with regional innovators sharing their work and connecting with professionals, students, and others in the ecosystem.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/69e242d83883c4c0b24785a6_InnovateXXLmeetup-content2.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

##### The organizer of the event is INNOVATE, a platform for future builders, impact makers, and changemakers in the innovation hotspot region of Arnhem/Nijmegen.
