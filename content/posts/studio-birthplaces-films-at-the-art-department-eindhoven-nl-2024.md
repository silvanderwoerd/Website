# 'Broken Wings' and 'Wear Wool, Not Fossil Fuel' Screenings at The Art Department Eindhoven NL 2024 Festival and Conference

‍

We're delighted to announce that two of our projects will be part of The Art Department Eindhoven NL 2024's curated films showcase: "Change Your World - Purpose-driven Shorts".

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/662131e87542e96c2d6d24dc_content1.News.ArtDepartment.jpg.png"></div></figure>

Hosted by Playgrounds, The Art Department Eindhoven NL is a major festival and conference focusing on the design process and craftsmanship in film, animation and games. The 'Change Your World' short film showcase is part of their Film Festival lineup, with shorts film selected for their ability to inspire others to campaign for a better world. We're incredibly honored that two of our films were selected to be part of this.

‍

This is not the first time Studio Birthplace has been a part of Playgrounds' festivals, the last time being when co-founder and creative director Sil van der Woerd presented the studio’s work and mission at Playgrounds' BLEND 2022 Festival.
