## **Creators Inc swoops for Studio Birthplace. - DavidReviews**

‍

David Reviews has written an article about Studio Birthplace's signing with Creators Inc. for global representation.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/677cab63d5d8f7987e696376_677cab0d379097de2b98b175_Screenshot%25202025-01-07%2520121748.png"></div></figure>

‍

Here's an excerpt from their article:

‍

"When Sil van der Woerd and Jorik Dozy, founders of Studio Birthplace, consider a new project, they ask themselves one question: "Will this improve our world?"

‍

It's a lot of pressure to put on a script, but that's just how Studio Birthplace rolls.

‍

When The Woolmark Company approached them in 2022, Dozy and van der Woerd were shocked to learn that enough oil to fill an Olympic-sized swimming pool is used to make synthetic clothing.

‍

Thus, Studio Birthplace had a plucky cast emerge from a pool of black gold, which is also how many oil company executives are born."
