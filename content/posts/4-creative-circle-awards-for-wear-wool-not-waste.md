# Wear Wool, Not Waste wins across 4 categories - Gold Commercial Colour Grading, Silver Cinema (1 min & under), Bronze Film For Change and Bronze Sound Design.

‍

Our campaign film Wear Wool, Not Waste made for the Woolmark Company has won four awards at the Creative Circle Awards 2025.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/687e1266491cdf0548ae7025_Screenshot%202025-07-21%20070649.png" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

Wear Wool, Not Waste won under the following categories: 

Gold – Colour Grading | Commercial Film Grading

Silver – Film | Cinema (1 min & under)

Bronze – Film | Film for Positive Change

Bronze – Film Craft | Sound Design

‍

The Creative Circle is the longest running creative awards in Europe, now celebrating its 80th Anniversary. We're honored that the hard work of our crew has been recognized across it's four diverse award categories.
