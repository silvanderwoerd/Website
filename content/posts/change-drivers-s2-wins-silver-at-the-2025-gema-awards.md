# The 2025 GEMA Global Awards for TV and Streaming celebrate the bold creativity, relentless passion, and innovative spirit that define the global entertainment marketing community.

‍

Change Drivers Season 2 has won the Silver - Video Campaigns award under the Global category at The 2025 GEMA Global Awards for TV and Streaming.

‍

‍

The GEMA Awards, or Global Entertainment Marketing Academy Awards, recognize outstanding achievements in entertainment marketing across categories like TV, streaming, film, music, sports, and video games. It is a large, international awards program with regional competitions in areas like North America, Latin America, Europe, Asia, and Australia/New Zealand.

‍

We're honored that Change Drivers Season 2 has won Silver in the global category at the GEMA Awards.
