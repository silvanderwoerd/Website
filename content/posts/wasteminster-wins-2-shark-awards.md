# Greenpeace's Wasteminster has won 2 Shark Awards!

‍

We are delighted that Wasteminster, made for Greenpeace, has won two Shark Awards: a silver for Best Animation Direction and a silver for Charities & Trusts.

‍

‍

Huge congratulations to the whole team who worked tirelessly on such an urgent and crucial piece of work, helping to denounce the gravity and scale of the UK plastic waste exports.
