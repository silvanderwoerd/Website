# Studio Birthplace's creative director Sil van der Woerd spoke at the high level G7 ‘Digital Twins of The Oceans’ summit, at Lancaster House in London.

‍

In a room filled with some of the most established ocean scientists, Sil spoke about digital twins in film and games, about delivering information through emotion, and the most important stories of our time with impact.
