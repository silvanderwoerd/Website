# "This year's honourees have set the standard for creative excellence." - 2026 GEMA Awards Europe

‍

Change Drivers Season 3: Douglas McMaster has won Gold under the Social Responsibility category at this year's GEMA Awards Europe.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image" data-max-width="748"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6a264a6165f2eec5a9d56cd1_image001.png" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

The GEMA Awards, or Global Entertainment Marketing Academy Awards, recognize outstanding achievements in entertainment marketing across categories like TV, streaming, film, music, sports, and video games. It is a large, international awards program with regional competitions in areas like North America, Latin America, Europe, Asia, and Australia/New Zealand.

‍

Change Drivers S2 won Silver in the global category at the 2025 GEMA Awards and so we're delighted to bring home the gold this year.
