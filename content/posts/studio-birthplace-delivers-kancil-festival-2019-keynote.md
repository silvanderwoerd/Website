# Studio Birthplace was honored with the opportunity to speak at the 2019 edition of the Kancil Awards Festival in Kuala Lumpur, held October 24th-25th.

‍

Co-founders Jorik, Sil and Sean gave an hour-long keynote about their journey, filmmaking approach and beliefs behind Studio Birthplace.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/65e8aa9bf33a3516566b781f_content1.talks.kancil2019.jpeg" loading="lazy" alt=""></div></figure>

‍

The festival brings together the top of the commercial industry in the region and showcases a variety of fascinating speakers from across disciplines to inspire and challenge mind-sets.

‍

The theme of this year's festival was 'Belief Creates' and saw speakers like Ronald Rael talk about his activism work along the US-Mexico border, and Photographer James Delano present his heartbreaking work covering the ongoing drug war in the Philippines.
