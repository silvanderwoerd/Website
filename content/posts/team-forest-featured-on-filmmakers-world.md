## **Inside the making of WWF’s Team Forest, where stunt rigging, VFX and environmental storytelling came together high above the Indonesian rainforest canopy. - Filmmakers World**

‍

Filmmakers World has featured Team Forest on their website, after the social media posts for the project on their social platforms generated more than 2M views across FilmmakersWorld’s social channels.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image" data-max-width="2535"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6a799e5c1ab37e9a953d3fa3_Screenshot%202026-08-10%20054518.png" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

An excerpt from the article below: 

‍

"We first featured Studio Birthplace when we explored the craft behind Earth Defender and Wear Wool, Not Waste, two films that showcased the studio’s unique approach to blending filmmaking, VFX and environmental storytelling. If you missed that conversation, it’s the perfect place to start before diving into their latest work.

‍

Directors Sil van der Woerd and Jorik Dozy are now back with Team Forest, a new campaign for WWF that stages a football match across the Indonesian rainforest canopy. It also became one of the strongest-performing stories we’ve shared this year, generating more than 2M views across FilmmakersWorld’s social channels."

‍
