# The winning work for Shots APAC Awards was chosen by a cross-section of directors, creatives, producers, cinematographers, stylists and craft specialists from across the Asia Pacific region.

‍

Earth Defender has won Silver in the Best Ad of the Year: VFX category at this year's Shots Asia Pacific Awards.

‍

‍

The Shots Awards are a global series of awards that celebrate exceptional creativity, craft, and production excellence in advertising and film across different regions, such as the Asia Pacific region.

‍

Our hearfelt congratulations to VFX Partner on the film Heckler HQ for their outstanding work.

‍
