## **Pioneering directors and creative studio, Studio Birthplace, has signed to Creators Inc for global representation. - Shots**

‍

Shots Magazine has written an article about Studio Birthplace's new global representation with Creators Inc..

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image" data-max-width="2526"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/67541fec63a93e934f04d36f_67541fe185557b7a3273d89c_Screenshot%25202024-12-07%2520181327.png" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

‍

Here's an excerpt from their article:

‍

"Founded by directing duo Sil van der Woerd and Jorik Dozy, Studio Birthplace is dedicated to creating awareness and inspiring change in unique ways across a multitude of ecological and humanitarian issues. In the face of climate meltdown, massive inequity and a sixth mass extinction, the studio is committed to disrupt the way environmental and social stories are told.

‍

By breaking down the old ways of thinking with fearless creativity and endless curiosity, it shifts the narrative towards a just, healthy world for all, using the power of storytelling to inspire change."
