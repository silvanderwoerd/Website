# Jorik Dozy represents Studio Birthplace at Wildscreen's Science In Storytelling Event, where scientists and filmmakers come together in a symbiosis of minds to learn, collaborate and share how best to keep science at the heart of natural world stories.

‍

Held at the Bristol Beacon in Bristol, England, Jorik joined a distinguished panel made up of Rob Eagle - a Researcher in Media and the Cultural Industries, Reina-Marie Loader - a Principal Academic in Film Production and Francesca Panetta - a Director part of AKO Storytelling Institute, UAL for the 'Science OF Storytellling' Session.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/67e546a24bd1e476f73ceaef_Talk.WS.Content1.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

This panel examined behavioral science, psychology, and social impact can enhance natural world storytelling to drive climate action, foster a deeper connection with nature, and promote environmental stewardship as well as explored innovative narrative techniques and impact storytelling strategies designed to inspire meaningful behavior change and create lasting societal impact.

‍

On his time at Wildscreen, Jorik says:

‍

"Studio Birthplace’s mission is focused on reclaiming storytelling as a positive tool for change, so it was incredibly inspiring to see Wildscreen dedicate an entire festival to this. We need more spaces where creativity and action come together.”
