### Out now - 'Change Drivers Season 2, Episode 3: Merijn Tinga'.

‍

*Change Drivers Season 2: Episode 3* features Merijn Tinga, AKA the Plastic Soup Surfer.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/66df436b6f22640ab5da02f8_66df432028a0c965538b7dff_CD.Merijn.Still1.jpeg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

In this episode, Merijn advocates for us to transform our throwaway culture and demands a higher responsibility when it comes to how we use and discard plastics.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/66df436b6f22640ab5da02f5_66df432bc48f4a041982ce8a_CD.Merijn.Still2.jpeg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

*Change Drivers* is a mini-series created for Discovery's various channels highlighting people working on the front lines of solving critical problems the world faces today. From plastic pollution to climate change, the series aims to tell the stories of those who are putting everything on the line to find solutions to some of our society's most dire problems.

‍

The episode is currently on-air on Discovery Channel as well as TLC, Food Network, Travel Channel, Animal Planet and more.
