# Earth Defender will be screening in the iconic TCL Chinese Theatre, the same venue where the Oscars are held.

‍

Earth Defender has been selected under the Music Video category at the Oscar-qualifying Hollyshorts Film Festival.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/687e166db364c3849ccdd5ae_Screenshot%202025-07-21%20072737.png" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

The HollyShorts Film Festival is based in Los Angeles, California. Since its establishment in 2005, HollyShorts has grown into a premier platform for independent filmmakers worldwide.

‍

We were last at Hollyshorts for SYML's Flags, where we were selected for the same category. We're excited to return!

‍
