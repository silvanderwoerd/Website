# 1.4: 'The Medium is the Message'

‍

Woolmark's 'Wear Wool, Not Fossil Fuel' has been shortlisted in September 2022's 'Six of the Best Creative Commercials'.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/65e27e880b149fe32466ae8c_content.woolmark.14.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

1.4 has been showcasing the best established and new talent in short-form filmmaking since 2012. They are also known for their 'Six of the Best" features in numerous categories, from Creative Music Videos to Cannes Lion Film Crafts.<br><br>

The full list can be read [here](https://www.onepointfour.co/2022/09/14/six-of-the-best-creative-commercials-59/).
