We're incredibly honored to announce that Broken Wings has won its fifth award, this time for 1st Prize, The Àngel Rabat Graugés Award at the Environment and Sustainability Short Film Festival in the Montseny Natural Park and Biosphere Reserve, Spain.

‍

Although directors Sil van der Woerd and Jorik Dozy couldn't attend the ceremony due to their ongoing work on a special project in Kalimantan, Indonesia, they sent a thank-you video that was played during the festival's awards ceremony.
