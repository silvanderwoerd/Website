### Out now -  'Change Drivers Season 3, Episode 2: Christopher Raeburn'.

‍

*Change Drivers Season 3: Episode 1* follows Christopher Raeburn, a pioneering sustainable fashion designer.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image" data-max-width="1280"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6a06176eb6d87c17d6b82e62_CDChris-Still1.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

In this episode, Christopher challenges us through his work to see that waste is only waste when we fail to use our imagination.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image" data-max-width="1280"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6a061795e40e2e72ea414286_CDChris-Still2.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

From transforming discarded materials such as parachutes and maps into high value fashion, to championing second hand shopping and circular design, Christopher's pieces are proof on how sustainability and style doesn't have to be at odds.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image" data-max-width="1280"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6a0617ae1a0ad32df9db61d8_CDChris-Stil3.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

*Change Drivers* is a mini-series created for Discovery's various channels highlighting people working on the front lines of solving critical problems the world faces today. From plastic pollution to climate change, the series aims to tell the stories of those who are putting everything on the line to find solutions to some of our society's most dire problems.

‍

The episode is currently on-air on Discovery Channel and across Warner Bros. Discovery networks worldwide.
