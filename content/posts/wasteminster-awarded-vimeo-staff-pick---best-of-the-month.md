### Wasteminster is now officially a Vimeo Staff Pick - Best of The Month

‍

We are very happy to receive the Vimeo Staff Pick for Best Film of the Month in the Commercial category for Greenpeace's Wasteminster.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/65e897c85017a8de2be3e062_wasteminster_vimeostaffpick.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

The film can be viewed in the Best of Staff Picks channel and will proudly wear a Best of the Month badge forevermore.

Vimeo has always been an instrumental platform for filmmakers. To be handpicked this way amongst the best is a great honour, and we feel very encouraged to continue the work we do!
