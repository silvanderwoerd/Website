An excerpt from the article:

‍

MoneyTrees sees Greenpeace reunited with Sil van der Woerd and Jorik Dozy of Studio Birthplace, who it previously worked with on a commercial to raise awareness of the UK’s plastic waste.

‍

For this new ad, the directors stress that no trees were destroyed in its creation.

‍

“Our collaboration with Greenpeace draws on the power of art and activism to drive positive change. In portraying the forest destruction caused by bankers and corporations, it was crucial to us that no trees were harmed during filming. Luckily we found a location that featured two giant trees that had recently been floored by a storm, creating a natural ‘destruction’ site.”
