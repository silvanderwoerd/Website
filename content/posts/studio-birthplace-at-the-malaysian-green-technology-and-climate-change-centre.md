# Studio Birthplace's Sean Lin was invited to the Malaysian Green Technology and Climate Change Centre (MGTC)'s 2020 International Youth Day celebration on August 20th.

‍

As a Malaysian youth who has been having a major success in his field, Sean Lin was invited to share his experiences to inspire Malaysian youths to use creativity for a sustainable future.

‍

With the theme in mind, Sean gave a 10 minute presentation about Studio Birthplace's mission and creative approach to delivering information through emotion.

‍

‍

The theme of International Youth Day 2020 is “Youth Engagement for Global Action” and it seeks to highlight the ways in which the engagement of young people at the local, national and global levels is enriching national and multilateral institutions and processes. The sharing session was part of Malaysia's Expo 2020 Dubai Celebrates International Youth Day event, held at MGTC.  

‍

The aim of the event was to acknowledge the youth's butterfly effect towards improvement and empowerment of the community in our aim to achieve a sustainable world, especially in Malaysia.
