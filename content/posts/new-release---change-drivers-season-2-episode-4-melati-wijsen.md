### Out now - 'Change Drivers Season 2, Episode 4: Melati Wijsen'.

‍

*Change Drivers Season 2: Episode 4* follows founder of Youthtopia, Melati Wijsen.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/66df479754e3243986c3326f_66df44667aced2cdf7ee3280_CD.Melati.still1..jpeg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

In this episode, Melati closes off the second season with an important reminder that each of us can make a difference. She has been empowering people to create positive change ever since she was 12 years old. Now, she invites us all to join her.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/66df479754e3243986c33272_66df449f6b5012e172c250a4_CD.Melati.still2.jpeg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

*Change Drivers* is a mini-series created for Discovery's various channels highlighting people working on the front lines of solving critical problems the world faces today. From plastic pollution to climate change, the series aims to tell the stories of those who are putting everything on the line to find solutions to some of our society's most dire problems.

‍

The episode is currently on-air on Discovery Channel as well as TLC, Food Network, Travel Channel, Animal Planet and more.
