# Novo Amor ‘Birthplace’ video is a powerful statement for the oceans - Parley for the Oceans

‍

Parley for the Oceans is a nonprofit environmental organization that focuses on protection of the oceans, and they've decided to spotlight Birthplace on their website.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/65e8dcba6ae4a82598da88ce_content.birthplace.parley.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

Here's an excerpt from the interview:

‍

Ahead of the October release of his debut album ‘Birthplace,’ Novo Amor, aka Ali Lacey, is harnessing his talents to be a catalyst for collaboration and a voice for the oceans. His recently released video for ‘Birthplace,’ the album’s title track, stars world-renowned freediver Michael Board suspended in the stunning beauty of the living ecosystem — and then surrounded by its very real and growing threat: plastic pollution.
