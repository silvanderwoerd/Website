Our campaign Team Forest for WWF has been written about by Creative Review.

"While all eyes are on the World Cup for the next five weeks, WWF are taking the beautiful game somewhere it has never been before: a rainforest canopy.

‍

Created by Studio Birthplace, Team Forest transforms a stretch of Indonesian rainforest into a vertical football pitch, with players sprinting across the forest floor, scaling towering tree trunks and traversing high above the ground in a spectacular feat of filmmaking."
