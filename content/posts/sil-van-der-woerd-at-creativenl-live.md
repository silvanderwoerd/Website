# Sil's presentation of Studio Birthplace's work during CreativeNL Live's Session: On a New Crossroad received a standing ovation.

‍

Sil van der Woerd was a speaker at CreativeNL Live's session during Dutch Design Week where he presented Studio Birthplace's work and mission.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/68fe0ad44efeaf17e66995d7_20251022_CreativeNL_DDW_Photo-Ben-Houdijk_lr_7077.jpg"></div></figure>

CreativeNL Live's session: On A New Crossroad aims to spark conversations on how Dutch creatives can respond to the major shifts shaping our world.

‍

On his time there, Sil reflects:

‍

"Thank you CreativeNL for inviting me to speak on how the creative industries can lead cultural shifts by telling stories for our planet. My talk was met with a standing ovation. Working in a mostly digital space, it is rare to receive such a wave of energy from a crowd which was humbling and touching."

‍

‍

CreativeNL aims to bring together design thinkers and doers around the world to turn ideas into actions that contribute to a strong Dutch creative sector and more beautiful world.
