## Play now - Jungle Heroes is a free-to-play mobile strategy & resource management game in which players restore natural ecosystems & bring back wildlife.

‍

##### Studio Birthplace, Plant-for-the-Planet and Little Chicken Game Company have released Jungle Heroes. Every year, we lose 10 million hectares of forest, home to countless threatened species. But what’s the first job in many games? To chop a tree! What if there’s a game that flips that entirely? Both in-game and in the real world?

##### ‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-video"><div><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/SgqRy92ef1g" title="Jungle Heroes - Official Launch Trailer | Save the Rainforest 🌱" scrolling="no" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="true"></iframe></div></figure>

‍

##### Players remove pollution, replant rainforest trees, and harvest fruit to restore ecosystems and bring back threatened species. The resources also help repel intruders such as excavators and loggers. Along the way, players rehabilitate baby orangutans, and unlock new areas, stories, characters, and missions.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/69e5a147811fddf6f6e9b21f_Screenshot%201%20-%20Opening%20Scene.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

##### A portion of all in-app revenue is donated to Plant-for-the-Planet’s restoration projects. Progress updates are shared through the in-game newsfeed, keeping players engaged and turning the community into an active movement that contributes to real-world change.
