PlantBasedNews has released an article about Wasteminster.

An excerpt from the article:

‍

A new satirical animation shedding light on the pollution crisis shows [UK Prime Minister Boris Johnson](https://plantbasednews.org/culture/politics/vegan-doctors-urge-boris-johnson-to-adopt-plant-based-diet/) drowning in plastic. *Wasteminster* is directed by Jorik Dozy and Sil van der Woerd for environmental organization Greenpeace. It is voiced by acclaimed impressionists Jon Culshaw and Matt Forde and produced by Studio Birthplace.
