## Out now - To Love and Protect spotlights the power of Indonesian women restoring coral reefs through access, creativity, and community.

‍

##### *To Love and Protect* is a joint collaboration between Frames of Change and Studio Birthplace to highlight the work of women-led ocean protection organization Coral Catch.

##### ‍

##### More than 84% of the world's coral reefs have been subjected to intense, bleaching-level ocean heat stress. As Coral Catch marks five years of reef protection, they are showing us what can happen when we refuse to give up.

##### ‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6970c92f488355ef693a0f74_Stills-3.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

##### Based in Indonesia, Coral Catch aims to empower local women with access to the ocean, professional dive training, and leadership pathways in reef restoration.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6970c9eb49d92d925e05fcbb_Stills-2.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

##### Through community-led action, collaboration, and care, they are restoring reefs and building hope where it is needed most.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6970ca0d346d678546baa8ca_Stills-8.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

##### As conversations around coral reefs reaching critical tipping points became louder, Coral Catch's work has never felt more necessary.
