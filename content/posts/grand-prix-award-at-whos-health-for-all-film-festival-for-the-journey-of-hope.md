# The festival's aim is to recruit a new generation of film and video innovators to champion and promote global health issues.

‍

We're honored to announce that The Journey of Hope received the Grand Prix Award at The World health Organization's Health For All Film Festival under the Better Health and Well-Being category.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/65e896a43842886894be3921_content.flags.healthforall.jpeg"></div></figure>

‍

The WHO Health for All Film Festival invites independent filmmakers, production companies, public institutions, NGOs, communities, students, and film schools from around the world to submit their original short films on health.
