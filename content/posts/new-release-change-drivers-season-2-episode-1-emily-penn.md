### Out now - 'Change Drivers Season 2, Episode 1: Emily Penn'

‍

*Change Drivers Season 2: Episode 1* revisits ocean advocate and skipper Emily Penn, this time as she champions the fight against microplastics.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6630db6df230226872e970ec_CDS2E1.Stills1.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

As an expectant mother for the second time, she's voicing her concerns about household plastic use, particularly with kids in the picture. Join her in discovering practical solutions to keep your home plastic-free and safe for your little ones.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6630db2a2829aa507eb65846_CDS2E1.Stills2.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

*Change Drivers* is a mini-series created for Discovery's various channels highlighting people working on the front lines of solving critical problems the world faces today. From plastic pollution to climate change, the series aims to tell the stories of those who are putting everything on the line to find solutions to some of our society's most dire problems.

‍

The episode is currently on-air on Discovery Channel as well as TLC, Food Network, Travel Channel, Animal Planet and more.
