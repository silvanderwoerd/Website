## **Heckler Singapore Wins VFX Gold and Best Of category at Creative Circle Awards - LBB Online**

‍

Little Black Book has published an article celebrating Heckler Singapore's fantastic night at GONG Awards 2025, where amongst numerous awards they won Gold VFX and Best of Category: Craft - Film and Branded Entertainment for Moneytrees.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/67960c951cade9c1591c857c_67960c480964bad836a3dd53_moneytrees.gongawardsLBB.Content1.jpeg" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

An excerpt from the article below:

‍

Led by Heckler Singapore creative director Cody Amos, Greenpeace ‘MoneyTrees’ saw the VFX specialist team join forces with Studio Birthplace directors Jorik Dozy and Sil van der Woerd to create unforgettable visual effects on the striking music video.

‍

Launching on International Biodiversity Day, the music video cautions audiences with a stern wakeup call to end the corporate destruction of nature.

‍

Cody Amos says, "Gold and Best of Category for VFX! Suffice to say, we are all really chuffed.

‍

Big thanks to the team at Studio Birthplace for trusting us with such a playful but meaningful project.
