We're grateful to announce that Broken Wings has collected its 7th award this festival run, this time for Best International Short Film Award at 14th Edition of The Barichara Green Film Festival FESTIVER.

‍

FESTIVER was created by Colombian actors Toto Vega and Nórida Rodríguez, seeking to build a new generation of artists and audience commited to the environment. We're delighted for our film to be recognized by a festival with these values.
