# Broken Wings scores its first award at Lanzarote International Film Festival.

‍

We're delighted to announce that Broken Wings has won its first award, the Audience Award at Lanzarote International Film Festival.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image" data-max-width="1024"><a href="https://www.festivaldecinedelanzarote.com/en" target="_blank" class="w-inline-block"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/665dfd3346c8f262890d1585_award.brokenwings.content1.jpg" loading="lazy"></div></a></figure>

‍

Founded in 2001, Lanzarote International Film Festival is a Goya-qualifying film festival based in Lanzarote of the Canary Islands.
