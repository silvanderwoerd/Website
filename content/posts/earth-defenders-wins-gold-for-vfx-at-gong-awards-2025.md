# ​The Association of Advertising and Marketing Singapore (AAMS) celebrated Singapore’s creative excellence at the Gong Awards 2025, presenting 170 awards across 13 categories.

‍

Earth Defender has won under the Craft: Gongs - Film / Branded Entertainment: Visual Effects category at the 2025 Gong Awards.

‍

‍

‍

The Gong Awards,  is Singapore's premier creative award show, celebrating outstanding advertising, design, and marketing communications. Organized by the Association of Advertising & Marketing Singapore (AAMS), it serves as a benchmark for local creative standards, recognizing talent across agencies of all sizes, in-house teams, and individual creators.

‍

Our congratulations and gratitude to our crew such as our VFX Partner on the film Heckler HQ  for securing this win!
