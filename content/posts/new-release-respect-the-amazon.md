### Out now - Greenpeace's 'Respect The Amazon' is an animated short where paradise in the Amazon is ravaged by machines driven by profiteering executives until local defenders rise and spark a global movement.

‍

*'Respect the Amazon'* is our latest collaboration with Greenpeace, serving as a reminder that the Amazon cannot wait.

‍

The destruction of the Amazon is not new. But while the world looks elsewhere, the forest continues to burn, trees continue to fall, and Big Ag corporations like JBS profit from distraction.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/68bb37c79e14a94187a60d65_Greenpeace.JBS.STILLS_3.1.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

At the centre of the destruction sit the Batista Brothers, bloated executves feasting on steak and money, indifferent to the devastation they’ve wrought.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/68bb38cca06e81d947a2390a_Greenpeace.JBS.STILLS_7.1.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

But the forest is not silent! From its depths, local defenders rise, children, elders, protectors, their footsteps stirring the earth.

‍

The animated short calls us all to join them in holding the line.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/68bb39051badea0049028e90_Greenpeace.JBS.STILLS_12.2.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

This November at COP30, political leaders, surrounded by corporate lobbyists, will meet in the heart of the Brazilian Amazon, to decide the future of the planet. The message they need to know is clear: **Respect the Amazon**.

‍

This animated short is part of Greenpeace's international campaign *'Respect the Amazon',* with the participation of many Greenpeace offices including Brazil, Spain, The Netherlands among others.
