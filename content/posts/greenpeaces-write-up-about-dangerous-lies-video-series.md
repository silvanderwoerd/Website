# "#RealZero video series shows how offsetting is the biggest book keeping trick of the century."

‍

Greenpeace International has released a write-up about Dangerous Lies video series and the importance of hitting #RealZero.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/65e760c7e15cd8443357564c_content.news.dangerouslies.jpg"></div></figure>

An excerpt from the write-up below:

‍

Greenpeace International launched a Truman Show-style video series to highlight the dire consequences of falling for the world’s biggest polluters’ promises to “offset” their carbon emissions, hoping to inspire people to force the powers-that-be to take real climate action. Big polluters keep promising to take climate action, but continue polluting.

‍
