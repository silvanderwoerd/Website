### Out now - 'Earth Defender', a haunting music film that **exposes the life that is lost while defending nature, has premiered at COP29 in Baku, Azerbaijan.**

‍

*'Earth Defender'* captures the Iban community’s commitment to preserving their lands, set against artist Novo Amor's haunting score. Through its story, Earth Defender underscores the need for global support and policy change to ensure these protectors’ safety.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/673aa6ae55337a9ba4c80852_673aa45e0b330ab299997606_newrelease.earthdefender.still1.jpeg"></div></figure>

‍

Every 48 hours an Earth Defender goes missing or is murdered while protecting Earth's vanishing wilderness - our life support system.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/673aa6ae55337a9ba4c8084f_673aa4cdf4aef1c1a467ad37_newrelease.earthdefender.still2.jpeg"></div></figure>

‍

This music film is a a joint director collaboration between Studio Birthplace's Jorik Dozy and Sil van der Woerd with 19 year old Indigenous filmmaker [**Kynan Tegar**](https://www.instagram.com/kynantegar/?igsh=Njh5MHAzeWsyMWN6).

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6818acdae5050ada51e11937_EarthDefender.Premiere.Content1.jpg"></div></figure>

The directors premiered *Earth Defender* at Extreme Hangout during COP29 held in Baku, Azerbaijan. They also hosted a second screening during the Climate x Culture Forum held by The Convergence and Entertainment + Culture Pavilion at Baku Planetarium.[](https://www.linkedin.com/company/entertainment-culture-pavilion/)

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/6818adbbc4508ac0312d09aa_EarthDefender.Premiere.Content2.jpg"></div></figure>

‍

Supported by campaign partners Aliansi Masyarakat Adat Nusantara (AMAN), Yayasan HAKA and Global Alliance, the film urges audience that Immediate action is needed to safeguard Indigenous rights to land, culture, and environmen with a call to sign AMAN's petition for The Indigenous People's Bill, which aims to protect Indigenous peoples from losing their ancestral lands to government and corporate projects.
