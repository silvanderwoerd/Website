# The One Show is the world's most prestigious award show in advertising and design with a rich legacy of honoring some of the most groundbreaking ideas, created by some of the most remarkable minds in creativity.

‍

We're delighted to announce that Earth Defender has won a Bronze Pencil Award for Cinematography (Single) and a Merit Award for Visual Effects at this year's The One Show Awards.

‍

‍

We’re honoured that this story and the care behind its craft has been recognised by such an institutition.

‍

Our congratulations and gratitude to our crew such as our VFX Partner on the film Heckler HQ and Director of Photography Nicholas Chin for securing these awards.
