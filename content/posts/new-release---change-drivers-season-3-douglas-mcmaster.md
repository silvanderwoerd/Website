### Out now -  'Change Drivers Season 3, Episode 1: Douglas McMaster'.

‍

*Change Drivers Season 3: Episode 1* follows Douglas McMaster, a pioneering chef, food waste activist, and founder of the world’s first zero-waste restaurant.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/69c56a97af3f972077079b71_CD-Douglas-Still1.avif" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

In this episode, Douglas challenges something we’ve all accepted as normal: waste.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/69c56aa61558a4278209405c_CD-Douglas-Still2.avif" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

Douglas shares how rethinking our relationship with food can spark meaningful environmental change and how bold ideas can reshape entire industries. From fermentation to full-system redesign, Douglas shows that if nature wastes nothing, why should we?

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/69c56aeeae3a80d7820b9e22_CD-Douglas-Still3.avif" loading="lazy"></div></figure>

‍

*Change Drivers* is a mini-series created for Discovery's various channels highlighting people working on the front lines of solving critical problems the world faces today. From plastic pollution to climate change, the series aims to tell the stories of those who are putting everything on the line to find solutions to some of our society's most dire problems.

‍

The episode is currently on-air on Discovery Channel and across Warner Bros. Discovery networks worldwide.
