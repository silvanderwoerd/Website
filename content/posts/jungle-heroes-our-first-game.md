### Jungle Heroes is part of this year's Green Game Jam, the flagship initiative organized by Playing for the Planet Alliance.

‍

For a few years, we have been developing our very own game: Jungle Heroes, an exciting, free-to-play mobile strategy & resource management game in which players **restore ecosystems** and **bring back wildlife**.

‍

<figure class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img loading="lazy" alt="" src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6465c08bc2eb8d26649eae4b/66df1443e82fac979da1911e_66df10dae69180dcc98e3f71_JungleHeroes.GreenGamJam.MainImage.jpeg"></div></figure>

‍

Today, we're excited to announce that Jungle Heroes is part of this year's Green Game Jam - an annual challenge for games to build green activations. [The Green Game Jam](https://www.playing4theplanet.org/green-game-jam-2024) is the flagship initiative organized by [Playing for the Planet](https://www.playing4theplanet.org/) alliance, founded in 2019 at the UN Climate Action Summit in New York, the initiative is facilitated with the support of the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP).

‍

Jungle Heroes will be rolling out globally over the next few weeks. Today, the game is available on the [Apple App store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jungle-heroes/id1523046437) in 6 selected countries: Canada, France, Netherlands, Philippines, UK, USA. **‍**

‍
