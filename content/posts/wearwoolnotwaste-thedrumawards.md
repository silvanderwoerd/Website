# Wear Wool, Not Waste wins a Gold Creative Award and a Silver Sustainability & Responsibility Award at The Drum Marketing EMEA Awards.

‍

Our campaign film Wear Wool, Not Waste made for the Woolmark Company has won its next two awards at this year's The Drum Marketing EMEA Awards.

‍

‍

Wear Wool, Not Waste won Gold under the Creative category and Silver in the Sustainability & Responsibility category.

‍
