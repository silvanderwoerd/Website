# Sil van der Woerd was invited to be part of the 2025 Climate Leaders Forum held at the Gherkin, London.

‍

Sil van der Woerd was part of the 2025 Climate Leaders Forum in London, where he gave a Lightning Talk on Studio Birthplace's mission and creative approach to driving climate action.
