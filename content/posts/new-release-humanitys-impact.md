### Out now - Humanity's Impact: How many plastic bottles do we produce?

‍

*Humanity's Impacy: How Many Plastic Bottles Do We Produce* is about how our planet is flooded with single use plastics such as plastic bottles. Did you know that globally, we produce about 1 million plastic bottles every minute? What does that number even look like? This is what you're about to find out in Humanity's Impact.
