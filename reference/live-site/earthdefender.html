The world must listen:

Protect Defenders!

The Indigenous People's Bill to protect Indigenous peoples from losing their ancestral lands to government and corporate projects must urgently be passed. Despite a 2012 court ruling, formal protections remain absent, leaving Indonesia's Indigenous Peoples vital forests vulnerable to intimidation, criminalization, and land loss.

Immediate action is needed to safeguard Indigenous rights to land, culture, and environment. Your voice makes a difference. Demand real action now!

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Earth Defender - a haunting music film by Novo Amor and Studio Birthplace, exposes the life that is lost while defending nature.

Set against Novo Amor's haunting score, the film captures the Iban community’s commitment to preserving their lands. Through its story, Earth Defender underscores the need for global support and policy change to ensure these protectors’ safety.
