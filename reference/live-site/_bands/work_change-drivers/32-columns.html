Sagarika Sriram

Meet Sagarika, a young Eco Activist who has become a powerful environmentalist voice in the Middle East. She was only 11 years old when she created her ‘Kids for a Better World’ website, a digital platform that has since than connected a global, young community of nearly 100,000 like-minded peers who want to learn how they, too, can fight climate change.
