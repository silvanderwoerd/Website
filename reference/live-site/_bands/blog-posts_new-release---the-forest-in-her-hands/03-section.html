Out now - 'The Forest in Her Hands' reveals a story of restoration, care and the quiet power of those working to protect one of the planet's richest ecosystems we have left.

‍

Through the hands of Hutan’s Indigenous Orang Sungai women, a damaged rainforest begins to find balance again.

‍

From its beginnings as an orangutan research project, Hutan works to protect threatened wildlife and restore the habitats they depend on. Combining decades of scientific research with community-led conservation, Hutan is working toward a future in which people and wildlife can continue to coexist within one of Borneo’s most extraordinary landscapes, a habitat vital to the Earth's biodiversity.
