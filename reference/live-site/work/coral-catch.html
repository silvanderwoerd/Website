To Love and Protect

ABOUT THE PROJECT

To Love and Protect spotlights the power of Indonesian women restoring coral reefs through access, creativity, and community.

As ocean temperatures reach deadly thresholds, woman-led coral restoration organization Coral Catch shows us what’s possible when we refuse to give up.

Care for the Ocean

More than 84% of the world's coral reefs have been subjected to intense, bleaching-level ocean heat stress. This year, as conversations around coral reefs reaching critical tipping points became louder, Coral Catch's work has never felt more necessary.

‍

As Coral Catch marks five years of women-led marine conservation in Indonesia, they empower women once excluded from the ocean through professional dive training, reef restoration skills, and leadership pathways.

‍

When women are given access, conservation becomes stronger and more resilient.

Get Involved

Coral Catch aims to support 100 women leaders protecting Indonesia's reefs by 2030.

Frames of Change

This impact film for Coral Catch was a self-funded initiative brought to life through a collaboration between Studio Birthplace and Frames of Change.

‍

Frames of Change is a global filmmaking initiative created by filmmaker Jakub Chlouba to support grassroots organizations through meaningful films and to help filmmakers grow through real, hands-on education.
