What We Stand For

Our Values & Ethics

1

Independence & Integrity

Studio Birthplace is independent. We stand firm with those who engage in meaningful action and avoid those who greenwash. We work with integrity, responsibility and rely on local knowledge to guide us in environments we are unfamiliar with.

2

Collaboration & Respect

We believe in meaningful relationships both within our tightly-knit, global team and with our clients. We combat discrimination and encourage equality and diversity amongst our team and crews on our productions.

3

Awareness to Social Inequity

We work all over the world and are blessed to meet many different cultures, religions and people from all walks of life. We are very cautious not to take advantage of local communities. Instead, we look for ways to improve the lives of those we encounter.
